Raw Analyzer

  • Open: button, Ctrl+O, or drag a file onto the window.
  • Zoom: mouse wheel at the cursor, + / - keys, 0 toggles fit and 100%, double click fits.
  • Pan: drag with the Pan tool, or hold Space and drag with any tool.
  • Select: drag with the Select tool, or Shift+drag. The histogram switches to the selection. Esc clears it.
  • WB pick: click a neutral (grey or white) area to set the preview white balance.
  • Clipped marks 2x2 blocks where any channel reaches saturation (magenta). Shadows marks blocks whose weakest colour channel is below the chosen number of read-noise sigmas (blue). Red and blue usually sit a stop or more below green, so they reach the noise floor first. Read noise comes from the masked sensor border, or is estimated from the darkest image areas.
  • All statistics are computed on the undemosaiced raw values. The quick preview shows each 2x2 CFA block as one pixel. From 100% zoom the visible area is demosaiced with the algorithm chosen under Detail ≥100% (AHD by default; PPG and bilinear are faster), computed on several CPU threads.
  • Export image saves the image with the current white balance and exposure as JPEG, PNG or 16-bit TIFF, in sRGB or Adobe RGB with an embedded ICC profile.
  • Supported: Canon CRW (CIFF), CR2, CR3 (lossless and C-RAW), EOS-1D / 1Ds TIF.

Decoders ported from dcraw and LibRaw (LGPL 2.1 / CDDL 1.0).
